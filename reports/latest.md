## How to read this
- **In Australia** = an Australia-only region in the provider's docs, AWS in-region or its Australian Geo profile in Sydney/Melbourne, or a provider that says all its serving is in Australia (ResetData, Xinference). Provider claims haven't been checked from outside.
- **Australia + overseas** = SCX lists the model for AU and other regions, and its docs don't say how to pin a request to AU.
- **Overseas only** = offered, but processed outside Australia (e.g. SCX models not in its AU table, AWS Global-only models).
- Scores are a snapshot of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index; its versions are rescaled from time to time, so compare ranks rather than raw points across versions.
- **To get live scores:** add a free `ARTIFICIAL_ANALYSIS_API_KEY` to `~/open-inference/.env`.
- Model scores: Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai).
