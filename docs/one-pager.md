# Open Inference: One Pager `[Caesey 2026-11-04]`
_Caesey's own rewrite, done by hand after reading the Claude Doc, pasted into Claude Code on 2026-11-04. Kept word for word. This is the current version of the plan and replaces the drafts in [open-inference-plan](plan-notes.md). Claude's notes on it are at the bottom, labelled separately._

4 Nov 2026 · Caesey Fraser

## Why it exists
- The best open AI models are free to download, but most don't run on Australian computers.
- Only 1 or 2 of the top 27 open models run here, across the three providers checked.
- The gap hasn't moved since we found it in August.
- Stored in Australia doesn't mean processed in Australia.
- The top 8 open models come from Chinese labs, so running them here matters.
- People already pay for local AI: SCX reports A$6.5M a year from 49 customers.
- New models keep landing, so the job never ends.

## What we track every day
- Open models: every top open model, added as soon as it lands.
- Providers: every Australian and NZ GPU provider, added as soon as they appear.
- Missing list: the best models you can't get processed here.
- Model map: every closed and open model you can use here, and where it really runs.
- GPU prices: what each GPU costs per hour and per month in AUD, and who has stock.
- Chips: what each chip costs landed here, who sells it, and how long the wait is.
- Data centres: who's building what and where, announced versus live.
- Power: what electricity costs in each state.
- Market: who buys AI compute here, and what rules they follow.

## What we work out
- Cost to run: what every open model costs on every Australian GPU provider.
- Break-even: how much use a model needs to pay for itself.
- Australian markup: the local price versus the global price for the same model.
- Own, rent or sell: which makes the most money in Australia, in AUD.
- Who gets paid: how the money splits between chips, data centres, GPU providers and AI sellers.
- Cheapest spot: the best place in Australia to run AI.
- Provider ratings: a scorecard on price, stock, ownership, security and whether data stays here.
- Speed tests: how fast and reliable each service is from Australian and NZ cities.
- State of AI Compute report: all of it in one regular report.

## How we make money
1. Free data: daily data and posts build trust and an audience.
2. Paid data: deeper numbers and cost models for people making big decisions.
3. Advice: we get paid to guide businesses in Australia, NZ and Asia Pacific.
4. Private model hosting: customers pick a model and an Australian GPU provider, and we set it up.
5. Pay-as-you-go AI: we run the missing models here on partner GPUs and charge for use.

## Where it goes
- Start: one person and AI, putting out the data.
- Grow: partners and staff join as the business grows.
- Run: we become the company that runs these models end to end.
- Own: we own GPUs and data centres in Australia, NZ and Southeast Asia.

## The rules
- Fully bootstrapped, and Caesey keeps the equity.
- No GPU commitments until someone is paying.
- Stay agile: no label, we're just Open Inference.
- Get close to every provider, and keep the numbers honest.

## First moves
1. Automate the daily report across every Australian provider.
2. Collect GPU prices, online and by talking to providers.
3. Work out what every model costs to run here.
4. Rebuild the private model hosting product.
5. Put the data out publicly and start talking to people.

**Biggest open question: who pays first?**

_Numbers checked 4 Nov 2026. The provider check covered SCX, ResetData and Xinference. Everything here can change._
