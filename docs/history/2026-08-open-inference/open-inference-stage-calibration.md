On 2026-08-29 Caesey pushed back on a response that ended with execution advice (get supplier quotes, rent a
GPU, sign a first customer) with: "we havent started the business yet."

**Why:** There is no company yet and he has not decided which version of the idea to build. Advice that assumes
the decision is made skips the actual work he's doing, which is figuring out what the company should be. He said
this in his context document too — the idea is "extremely new" and he is "still discovering what the company
should become."

**How to apply:** Default to analysis that sharpens the decision — economics, market structure, contradictions,
what would have to be true — over to-do lists and go-to-market sequencing. Free desk research and reasoning are
stage-appropriate; spending money, incorporating, committing to capacity and contacting suppliers are not, unless
he asks. Challenge is welcome (he asked for it); premature execution framing is not.
