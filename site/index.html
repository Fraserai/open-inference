How to read this

  • In Australia: the provider's docs list an Australia-only region for the model, AWS runs it in-region or through its Australian Geo profile (Sydney and Melbourne), or the provider says all its serving is in Australia (ResetData, Xinference). Those last claims haven't been tested from outside.
  • AU + overseas: listed for Australia and other regions, with no documented way to pin a request to Australia.
  • Overseas only: offered, but processed outside Australia.
  • Processed in Australia isn't the same as sovereign. AWS is a US company; some local providers sit in US-owned data centres. The owner column says who runs each one.
  • Scores: Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, snapshot of 23 Oct 2026 (today's live scores weren't pulled).
